About E.Court

E.Court

Your Premier Pickleball Destination

E.Court is more than just a pickleball court — it's a community hub where players of all levels come together to play, socialize, and have fun. Located in the heart of Gingoog City, we offer a world-class facility designed for both casual players and competitive athletes.

Whether you're a beginner picking up a paddle for the first time or a seasoned player looking for your next match, E.Court has something for everyone.
